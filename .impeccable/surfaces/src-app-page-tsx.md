# Homepage `/` — surface brief

Scope: homepage (Persuade) + six minimal route stubs it links to. Turkish, concept site, no verified company facts yet.

Audience: partners, community, job seekers, press per PRODUCT.md §4. Job: understand the company and enter the right journey in under three clicks. Constraints: locked palette (paper #FDFBF7, terracotta #964F4C), no invented claims, no fabricated metrics/projects/news, WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage reads as a geological survey of a company: layered strata, hairline rules, and dated honesty replace the category's cinematic stock-photo hero and metric tiles. What it refuses: the mining photo wall and the unverifiable KPI strip.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper #FDFBF7 ground; ink #201A18 editorial type (Manrope display, Inter body); terracotta #964F4C as the single seam accent (~10% of surface); hairline rules, no box shadows. The authored strata contour SVG is the recurring material, drawn large in the hero and echoed as small plates.

STORY: A visitor learns what a responsible mining company does, sees honest scope (Arama / Geliştirme / Üretim as lifecycle, no invented projects), and reaches Faaliyetler or İletişim within one viewport.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height paper hero; oversized ink H1 left; strata field drawn across the lower right; primary ink button "Faaliyetlerimizi Keşfedin" with text link "Hakkımızda"; sticky paper header carrying the wordmark. Signature interaction: strata seams draw themselves once on load, decorative only.

FORM: Brief-pinned direction — PRD §3.2 industrial editorial minimalism with the owner-locked palette. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
